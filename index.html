Jiannan Tian, PhD

Google Scholar·ORCiD·GitHub·CV (PDF)

Jiannan Tian joined Oakland University as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Fall 2025, and is seeking self-motivated Ph.D. students to start their study from Spring 2026 or later. His research covers scientific computing, large-scale data reduction, data analytics, in situ neural-hybrid data reconstruction, and understanding AI, etc. He is collaborating with many researchers to innovate in building efficient HPC/AI cyberinfrastructure.

During his Ph.D. study, he worked as a student intern at Argonne National Laboratory (ANL), and published peer-reviewed work at top-tier venues. He is also the leading developer of open-source pSZ/cuSZ (link), a reference design of GPU-accelerated scientific lossy compressor.

Ph.D. positions For students interested in Ph.D. study, please reach out to jtian+phdstudy'at'oakland.edu with a brief introduction, CV, and academic transcripts.
